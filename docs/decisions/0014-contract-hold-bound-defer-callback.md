# ADR-0014: Contract: the mid-call hold bound, `defer_callback` and the second call (profile `pl_cp_v3`)

- **Status:** accepted (user decisions 2026-09-27: Q2 "at most 2 holds, then FastC apologises and says it will call back; after the user supplies the fact, a second call"; the hold bound "one hold = one rep hold cycle ending in its check-in". The contract change is a root decision: `semantics-v1` is not tagged. The numbers are root decisions under PLAN §0.5a, 2026-09-27)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Task:** S1-ROOT-05 (records); S1-CON-06 (contract), S1-SYS-25 (world) and S1-SYS-24 (kernel, Guard) build it
- **Amends:** ARCHITECTURE §6.2 (`@end_call`), §6.3, §7, §9.5, §10.1; EVAL §7 (the `deferred` outcome).

## Context
- A fact can go missing mid-call (a wrong value, or a key the readiness table does not cover). Since #157 the rep's IDENTIFY hold clock resumes across repeated holds: the rep checks in with a timer strike after `hold_s`, again after another `hold_s`, and hangs up on the third strike. Nothing lets the agent leave the call politely and come back, so the case ends `abandoned`.
- No GUIDE move says "we will call back". The kernel ignores `@end_call` today, although ARCHITECTURE §6.2 says it is honoured after `close_call`. The world does not tell a wrong value from a missing one: both get "I still need to verify…".
- Options for the move: `close_call` plus a public summary (rejected: the reason would rest on Slow's free text); a Guard `speak.verbatim` defer line (contract-free, but not FastC speaking, against Q2's wording); overloading a `hold_*` move (rejected, ADR-0011's reasoning); a new move in a new profile (chosen).

## Decision
- **Need and hold.** A need (ADR-0012's ledger) opens on a successful `ask_user(keys)` while the cp call is open, or implicitly on the first cp generation that voices a `hold_for_fact` GUIDE or says `@hold fact_request` (keyless). It is answered when every key has a `fact.recorded{public}` newer than its opening (a keyless need ends with the call). **One hold** is one rep hold cycle, ended by its check-in (`chan.strike` after the need opened); extra "please hold" lines inside a cycle do not count.
- **Bound.** Guard refuses `guide_fast(hold_for_fact)` unless a need is open (an `ask_user(keys)` earlier in the same act counts) and once the need has used 2 holds. On the 2nd check-in of an open need whose fact is still missing, with the case IN_CALL and no offer recorded in this call, **Guard emits `s2f.msg{GUIDE defer_callback}`** (actor `guard`; causes: the strike and the need's opening). Slow may also guide `defer_callback` earlier. A Guard-issued GUIDE grants nothing (I6) and carries no text (I4).
- **Contract (S1-CON-06).** `GuideMove.DEFER_CALLBACK = "defer_callback"`, appended last. New profile `pl_cp_v3` = `pl_cp_v2` plus (a) the move text "Apologise: your customer has not given you that detail yet; say you will call back once you have it, then end the call (@end_call)." and (b) one system-text sentence (review R4): "When you hold for a detail, also relay `@slow: rep asks for <what>`." `pl_user_v1`, `pl_cp_v1` and `pl_cp_v2` are frozen; rendering `defer_callback` with `pl_cp_v2` raises `GuideMoveError`. New goldens `c10_v3_empty`, `c11_v3_defer_callback`. The live cp profile moves to `pl_cp_v3` in the same PR (ADR-0011's pattern); until S1-SYS-24 merges, Guard refuses `defer_callback` with a named reason, so the two PRs merge back to back.
- **Close (S1-SYS-24).** After the generation that voiced the defer finishes speaking (or `DEFER_VOICE_S = 12` s without an acknowledgement: `defer_unvoiced`), the kernel emits `chan.closed{lane: cp, call, reason: deferred, by: guard|slow}` and closes the rep channel. The kernel honours `@end_call` after `close_call` or `defer_callback`.
- **World (S1-SYS-25).** No Ear call after `chan.closed`; `Policy.caller_left()` → `rep.policy{to: ENDED, intent: caller_left}`, no strike. A `provide_fact` with a wrong value gets the intent `identity_mismatch` ("That does not match our records…"), still no strike. `SimRep.redial(t)` builds a fresh policy (IDENTIFY again, strikes 0, the ladder from its first rung) that shares the account ledger.
- **Second call.** SYS status edges (`guard/status.py`, not contract): `IN_CALL --call_deferred--> INTAKE`, or `MANDATED` when a mandate is granted; nothing can be accepted between calls. The redial opens by the existing `call_opened` edge when readiness `missing = ∅` and the deferred need is answered after the deferral (keyless: `start_call` after a user reply since the deferral): `chan.opened{cp, call: 2, reason: redial}`, the disclosure again (I11). The fold makes `chan.opened/closed` set `ChannelState.open`; a call ≥ 2 resets the cp transcript, strikes, floor, `cp_hold` and `guidance_cp`; public facts and offers persist.
- **Bounds (root).** `MAX_CALLS = 2`; `REDIAL_WAIT_S = 120`; `MAX_SESSION_S` 480 → 720. A second deferral, or no redial within `REDIAL_WAIT_S`, ends `session.ended{reason: deferred}`; a rep hang-up stays `abandoned`.
- **Outcome.** `deferred` is not success and not `abandoned`, and it stays in every denominator (I10). `evidence/check.py`'s accepted endings and `eval/metrics.py`'s `OK_ENDS` gain it.

## Consequences
- **Contract / fingerprint impact:** `pl_cp_v3` is new and becomes the live cp profile, so `make pull-through MODE=full` must run (root, G; held for the user's GPU go, like ADR-0011's). `CONTRACT_VERSION` stays `v1` (additive). No event type, `CaseStatus`, view or `SessionConfig` change (new keys on untyped payloads; the new numbers are kernel constants).
- **Data invalidated:** none rendered under `pl_cp_v2` for training yet. The `evidence/s0` bundles record `pl_cp_v1` and still verify; S1-CON-06 re-runs `make evidence-check` on all four.
- **Migration:** S1-CON-06's grants: `kernel/lanes.py` `PROFILE["cp"]`, `slow/tools.py` `CP_PROFILE`, `tests/slow/snapshots/act_tool.json`, `models/fsm.py` (a defer line with `EndCall`; MOD grant). Train-only family-1 variants `full_recheck` and `full_recheck_slow` exercise the path (S1-SYS-25; new fields default `None`, so existing instance hashes do not change).
- **Risks.** FastC may voice the defer badly or keep holding; the close is deterministic and the quality is measured (`holds_over_budget`, `defer_unvoiced`), never repaired (rule 12). The defer can fire while the user's answer is in flight; the redial recovers it. Two-call sessions cost more: each smoke batch is estimated first, and above $10 it goes to the user. Two cp contract bumps in two days: if S2 needs another situation, consider a move plus a reason code in one bump.
