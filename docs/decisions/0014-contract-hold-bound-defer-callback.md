# ADR-0014: Contract: the mid-call hold bound, `defer_callback` and the second call (profile `pl_cp_v3`)

- **Status:** accepted (user decisions 2026-09-27: Q2 "at most 2 holds, then FastC apologises and says it will call back; after the user supplies the fact, a second call", and the hold bound "one hold = one rep hold cycle ending in its check-in". The contract change is a root decision, since `semantics-v1` is not tagged; the numbers are root decisions under PLAN §0.5a, 2026-09-27)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Task:** S1-ROOT-05 (records); S1-CON-06 (contract), S1-SYS-25 (world) and S1-SYS-24 (kernel, Guard) build it
- **Amends:** ARCHITECTURE §6.2 (`@end_call`), §6.3, §7, §9.5, §10.1; EVAL §7 (the `deferred` outcome).

## Context
- A fact can go missing mid-call (a wrong value, or a key outside the readiness table). Since #157 the rep's IDENTIFY hold clock resumes across repeated holds: a timer strike after each `hold_s`, a hang-up on the third. Nothing lets the agent leave politely and call back, so the case ends `abandoned`.
- No GUIDE move says "we will call back". The kernel ignores `@end_call`, although ARCHITECTURE §6.2 says it is honoured after `close_call`. The world answers a wrong value and a missing one alike ("I still need to verify…").
- Options: `close_call` plus a public summary (rejected: the reason rests on Slow's free text); a Guard `speak.verbatim` line (contract-free, but not FastC speaking, against Q2); overloading a `hold_*` move (rejected, as in ADR-0011); a new move in a new profile (chosen).

## Decision
- **Need and hold.** A need (ADR-0012's ledger) opens on a successful `ask_user(keys)` while the call is open, or on the first cp generation that voices a `hold_for_fact` GUIDE or says `@hold fact_request` (keyless). It is answered when every key has a `fact.recorded{public}` newer than its opening. **One hold** is one rep hold cycle, ended by its check-in (a `chan.strike` after the need opened); extra "please hold" lines inside a cycle do not count.
- **Bound.** Guard refuses `guide_fast(hold_for_fact)` unless a need is open (an `ask_user(keys)` earlier in the same act counts) and once it has used 2 holds. On the 2nd check-in of an open need whose fact is still missing, with the case IN_CALL and no offer recorded in this call, **Guard emits `s2f.msg{GUIDE defer_callback}`** (actor `guard`; causes: the strike and the need's opening). Slow may guide `defer_callback` earlier. A Guard-issued GUIDE grants nothing (I6) and carries no text (I4).
- **Contract (S1-CON-06).** `GuideMove.DEFER_CALLBACK`, appended last. `pl_cp_v3` = `pl_cp_v2` plus the move text "Apologise: your customer has not given you that detail yet; say you will call back once you have it, then end the call (@end_call)." and one system-text sentence (review R4): "When you hold for a detail, also relay `@slow: rep asks for <what>`." `pl_user_v1`, `pl_cp_v1` and `pl_cp_v2` stay frozen (`pl_cp_v2` raises `GuideMoveError` on the new move). Goldens `c10_v3_empty`, `c11_v3_defer_callback`. The live cp profile moves to `pl_cp_v3` in the same PR; Guard refuses `defer_callback` with a named reason until S1-SYS-24, and the two merge back to back.
- **Close (S1-SYS-24).** After the generation that voiced the defer finishes speaking (or `DEFER_VOICE_S` = 12 s without an acknowledgement: `defer_unvoiced`), the kernel emits `chan.closed{lane: cp, call, reason: deferred, by: guard|slow}` and closes the rep channel. The kernel honours `@end_call` after `close_call` or `defer_callback`.
- **World (S1-SYS-25).** No Ear call after `chan.closed`; the rep's policy ends `caller_left`, no strike. A wrong `provide_fact` value gets the intent `identity_mismatch`, no strike. `SimRep.redial(t)`: a fresh policy sharing the account ledger.
- **Second call.** SYS status edges: `IN_CALL --call_deferred--> INTAKE` (or `MANDATED` with a granted mandate), so nothing is accepted between calls. The redial uses the existing `call_opened` edge once readiness holds and the need was answered after the deferral (keyless: `start_call` after a user reply): `chan.opened{cp, call: 2, reason: redial}` and the disclosure again (I11). The fold sets `ChannelState.open` from `chan.opened/closed`; call 2 resets the cp transcript, strikes, floor, `cp_hold` and `guidance_cp`, and keeps public facts and offers.
- **Bounds (root).** `MAX_CALLS` = 2, `REDIAL_WAIT_S` = 120, `MAX_SESSION_S` 480 → 720. A second deferral, or no redial in time, ends `session.ended{reason: deferred}`; a rep hang-up stays `abandoned`.
- **Outcome.** `deferred` is neither success nor `abandoned` and stays in every denominator (I10); `evidence/check.py` and `eval/metrics.py` accept it as an ending.

## Consequences
- **Contract / fingerprint impact:** `pl_cp_v3` is new and becomes the live cp profile, so `make pull-through MODE=full` must run (root, G; held for the user's GPU go). `CONTRACT_VERSION` stays `v1` (additive). No event type, `CaseStatus`, view or `SessionConfig` change.
- **Data invalidated:** none; no training data is rendered under `pl_cp_v2` yet, and `evidence/s0` (`pl_cp_v1`) still verifies (S1-CON-06 re-runs `make evidence-check` on all four).
- **Migration:** S1-CON-06's grants: `kernel/lanes.py` `PROFILE["cp"]`, `slow/tools.py` `CP_PROFILE`, `act_tool.json`, `models/fsm.py` (a defer line with `@end_call`). Family 1's train-only variants `full_recheck`/`full_recheck_slow` exercise the path; their new fields default to `None`, so instance hashes do not change.
- **Risks.** FastC may voice the defer badly or keep holding: the close is deterministic and the quality measured (`holds_over_budget`, `defer_unvoiced`), never repaired (rule 12). A defer can fire while the user's answer is in flight; the redial recovers it. Two-call sessions cost more (a batch above $10 goes to the user). Two cp contract bumps in two days: a further situation should be a move plus a reason code in one bump.
